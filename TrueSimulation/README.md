## 1. Overview & Core Philosophy

Most synthetic market simulators rely on geometric Brownian motion or random walk equations ($S_{t+1} = S_t \cdot e^{\mu \Delta t + \sigma \sqrt{\Delta t} Z}$) and retroactively overlay volume, technical indicators, or candlestick patterns. 

**TrueSimulation** operates on a fundamentally different paradigm: **strict microscopic emergence**.

Price does not move because an equation tells it to move. Price moves because **discrete market participants place aggressive and passive orders into a limit order book (LOB), and the matching engine executes them against resting liquidity.**

$$\mathbf{Participants} \longrightarrow \mathbf{Orders} \longrightarrow \mathbf{Limit\ Order\ Book} \longrightarrow \mathbf{Executed\ Trades} \longrightarrow \mathbf{Price\ Returns} \longrightarrow \mathbf{Candles} \longrightarrow \mathbf{SMC\ Structure}$$

Trading concepts—including **Fair Value Gaps (FVG)**, **Liquidity Sweeps**, **Breaks of Structure (BOS)**, **Changes of Character (CHoCH)**, and **Volume Delta**—are not hardcoded artifacts; they emerge organically from order flow interacting with realistic, clumpy order-book liquidity.

## 2. Quantitative Microstructure Engine

### A. Continuous Double Auction Matching Engine
* **Order Types**: `LIMIT` and `MARKET` orders across `BUY` and `SELL` sides.
* **Price-Time Priority (FIFO)**: Resting limit orders are queued by price level, then execution timestamp.
* **Deterministic Slippage**: Aggressive market orders consume resting limit depth level-by-level. A large buy market order sweeps through resting ask liquidity, shifting the best ask and the mid price upward.
* **Volume Delta**: Every trade explicitly tracks aggressor side (`is_buy_aggressor` vs. `is_sell_aggressor`), producing real Order Flow Volume Delta ($\Delta = V_{\text{buy}} - V_{\text{sell}}$).

### B. Hawkes Self-Exciting Trade Arrival Process
To reproduce endogenous **volatility clustering** and trade clustering observed in real financial markets (Mandelbrot fat tails, ARCH/GARCH dynamics), trade arrivals follow a univariate Hawkes process:

$$\lambda(t) = \mu + \sum_{t_i < t} \alpha \cdot q_i \cdot e^{-\beta (t - t_i)}$$

Where:
* $\mu$ is the baseline arrival rate per regime.
* $\alpha \cdot q_i$ excites trade arrival intensity proportionally to executed trade size $q_i$.
* $\beta = 0.88$ represents the exponential decay rate per tick.
* When institutional block orders interact with resting depth, the excitation cascades, naturally generating high-volume momentum bursts and volatility expansions.

### C. Psychological Round-Number Depth Clustering
Real-world limit order books exhibit pronounced depth clustering around psychological round numbers. TrueSimulation probabilistically weights resting liquidity:
* **$3.2\times$ depth multiplier** at whole dollars (`.00`).
* **$2.5\times$ depth multiplier** at half dollars (`.50`).
* **$1.8\times$ depth multiplier** at quarters (`.25`, `.75`).
* **$1.35\times$ depth multiplier** at dimes (`.10`).

### D. Stochastic Air Pockets
Between major liquidity pools, the simulator injects stochastic **air pockets** (22% probability of $0.35\times$ reduced depth on non-round price levels). When aggressive flow hits an air pocket, price slips rapidly through multiple price levels in a single tick, creating authentic impulse legs and Fair Value Gaps.

### E. Market Maker Adverse Selection & Inventory Skew
* **Adverse Selection Shading**: Market makers monitor incoming short-term aggressive flow (`recent_taker_flow`). Under toxic institutional flow, market makers widen their spreads and shade quotes outward by 1–3 ticks.
* **Avellaneda-Stoikov Inventory Skew**: Market makers adjust their quote reservation price based on accumulated inventory:
  $$r(s, q) = s - q \cdot \gamma \sigma^2$$
  Bids and asks shift to incentivize opposing order flow, preventing unbounded inventory accumulation.

The simulation features five distinct probabilistic agent archetypes:

| Participant | Order Types | Core Strategy & Microstructure Impact |

| **Institutional / Whales** | Large Market | Directional liquidity-consuming block sweeps that trigger rapid book displacement, spread widening, and momentum cascades. |

## 4. Hidden Market Regimes

The market operates under hidden, transitioning macroeconomic regimes. Observers cannot directly view the regime unless revealed:
* **Trending Bullish**: High buy momentum persistence, elevated institutional buy frequency.
* **Trending Bearish**: Heavy sell-side pressure, elevated institutional sell flow.
* **Range / Consolidation**: High market-maker activity, tight spreads, balanced two-sided liquidity.
* **High-Volatility Expansion**: Wide spreads, reduced depth, frequent large sweeps, rapid price velocity.
* **Low-Volatility Compression**: Ultra-tight spreads, dense order books, low participant turnover.
* **Liquidity Drought**: Extremely thin order books where modest orders produce outsized slippage.

## 5. Emergent Smart Money Concepts (SMC)

All structural indicators are derived from candle mathematics and execution history:

* **Fractal Swing Highs & Lows**: Detected using configurable rolling lookback/lookforward windows.
* **Break of Structure (BOS)**: Formed when candle closes breach the prior swing high in an uptrend or swing low in a downtrend.
* **Change of Character (CHoCH)**: Formed when candle closes break the opposite structural swing point, indicating trend transition.
* **Fair Value Gaps (FVG)**:
  * *Bullish FVG*: $\text{Low}(\text{Candle}_{t}) > \text{High}(\text{Candle}_{t-2})$
  * *Bearish FVG*: $\text{High}(\text{Candle}_{t}) < \text{Low}(\text{Candle}_{t-2})$
  * Dynamically tracked for fill status, re-entry touches, and complete mitigation.
* **Liquidity Pools & Sweeps**: Buy-Side Liquidity (BSL) above swing highs and Sell-Side Liquidity (SSL) below swing lows. Sweeps dynamically resolve into either **reversals** (if absorbed by opposing limit liquidity) or **continuations** (if aggressive flow breaks through).

## 8. Installation & Quick Start

### Prerequisites
* Python 3.10+ (tested up to Python 3.14)

### 1. Install Dependencies
```bash
cd TrueSimulation
pip install -r requirements.txt
```

### 2. Launch Interactive Web Trading Terminal
```bash
python run_server.py
```
Open your browser and navigate to **`http://127.0.0.1:8000`**.

* Press **Spacebar** to Play / Pause.
* Press **Right Arrow** to step single ticks.
* Select playback speed up to **500x ⚡**.
* Open **Settings ⚙** to customize starting price, candle timeframe (15s, 30s, 1m, 5m), volatility scale, and simulation duration.

### 3. Run Headless Simulation from CLI
You can execute full simulations directly from the command line:

```bash
# Run 3,600 ticks with 60-second candles
python run_simulation.py --ticks 3600 --timeframe 60 --seed 42
```
